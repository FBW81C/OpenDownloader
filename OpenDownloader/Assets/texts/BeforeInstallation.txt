Welcome to the OpenDownloader Installer!

OpenDownloader is a simple free and open-source Video Downloader for Windows by FBW81C.

GitHub: https://github.com/FBW81C/OpenDownloader
YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@FBW81C

Features:
- Downloading Videos
- Default path
- FPS selection
- Quality selection
- Estimated file size
- ETA

Dependencies:
- .NET 8 (Core)
- ytdlp
